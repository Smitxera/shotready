I'm a beginner. Is this too advanced?

No. Each sheet starts with the exact numbers to set, so you can get the shot before you understand the theory. The Core skills sheets explain the basics in plain English when you're ready.

No. It's a digital download (PDF). Print the sheets at home or at any print shop, or keep them on your phone.

No. Once downloaded or printed, the sheets work offline: on a mountain, on a boat or on a plane.

Are the settings exact?

They're proven starting points. Light changes from scene to scene, so every sheet tells you which setting to adjust first if your shot is too dark, too bright or blurry.
